TRADE WITH A PLAN

Risk Management

A clear plan can help you make more consistent decisions. Every trade still carries risk, even with an analysis tool.

↗ How analysis can help

  • Organize recent market data and make patterns easier to review.
  • Compare selected contracts and apply entry rules consistently instead of relying only on emotion.
  • Track results, position sizes and stop settings so you can review your decisions.

! Know the limitations

  • Past frequencies and patterns do not guarantee future outcomes. A high historical win rate is not proof of an edge.
  • Payouts and costs matter: many small wins can be outweighed by fewer large losses.
  • Martingale can rapidly increase stakes and losses. Fast or automated trading can also magnify losses quickly.
  • Stop-loss thresholds are not guaranteed loss caps; a trade may settle beyond the limit.

✓ A practical approach

Decide what you can afford to lose, choose a stake and limits that fit your plan, and test in a demo before considering real funds. Review both winning and losing trades.
